## Overview

The step from an idealised $\lambda$ to a number a forecast centre can act on. Lorenz (1982) showed how to estimate predictability directly from an operational forecast archive using *lagged* forecasts — the difference between a one-day and a two-day forecast valid at the same time estimates error growth without ever knowing the truth. The chapter also confronts why measured atmospheric doubling times (~1.5–2 days) are so much longer than the fastest instabilities in the equations.

## The planned notebook

The lagged-forecast estimator applied to synthetic forecasts from Lorenz 96, where the truth *is* known, so the estimator can be validated before it is trusted on real data.
