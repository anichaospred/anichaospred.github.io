## Overview

Lorenz (1969) asked what happens when a system has many scales of motion, each with its own error-growth rate, and reached a conclusion far more troubling than the butterfly effect: if small scales grow fast enough, error introduced at the smallest scale contaminates the largest in a **finite** time no matter how small it starts. That is an intrinsic limit, and no observing system can defeat it.
