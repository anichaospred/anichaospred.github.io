## Overview

Low-order chaos and fully developed turbulence are not the same phenomenon, and the difference matters for predictability. A dissipative system with a low-dimensional attractor has a handful of positive exponents; a turbulent flow has a continuum of active scales and an error spectrum that evolves. This chapter connects the two pictures in two-dimensional and quasi-geostrophic flow.
