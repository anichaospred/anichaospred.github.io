## Overview

The book has used time averages and ensemble averages interchangeably. This chapter says when that is legitimate. An invariant measure is the mathematical object 'climate' actually refers to, ergodicity is the property that makes a single long run informative about the ensemble, and both can fail in exactly the cases — multiple attractors, non-stationary forcing — that Part VI cares about most.

## The planned notebook

Time average against ensemble average on Lorenz 63, and then on a bistable system where the two visibly disagree.
