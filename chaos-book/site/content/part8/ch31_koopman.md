## Overview

A nonlinear system on a finite-dimensional state space is equivalent to a *linear* operator acting on an infinite-dimensional space of observables. That trade — nonlinearity for dimension — underlies dynamic mode decomposition and a growing body of data-driven forecasting and assimilation work, including the `quantum-koopman-da` line of research.

DMD on Lorenz 63: how well a finite dictionary of observables reproduces the dynamics, and how the approximation degrades as the horizon lengthens.
