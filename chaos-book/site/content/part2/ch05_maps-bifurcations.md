## Overview

The logistic map, $x_{n+1} = r x_n(1-x_n)$, is one line of arithmetic and contains the whole period-doubling route to chaos: a stable fixed point gives way to a two-cycle, then a four-cycle, then eight, with the windows shrinking by a universal factor $\delta \approx 4.669$ that is the same for an enormous class of systems. The reason this belongs in a weather and climate book is **regime transitions**: a bifurcation is what it looks like when a slowly changing parameter causes a qualitative change in behaviour.
