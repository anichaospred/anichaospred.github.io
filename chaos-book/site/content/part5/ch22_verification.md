## Overview

Where the book's opening question gets its operational answer. Anomaly correlation, the conventional 0.6 threshold, and the historical record of forecast skill — which shows the **practical** horizon advancing by roughly a day per decade while the **intrinsic** horizon has not moved at all. The chapter closes by asking how much practical improvement is left.
