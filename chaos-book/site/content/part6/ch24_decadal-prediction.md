## Overview

The ocean's heat capacity is the climate system's memory, and it is what makes initialised prediction meaningful on interannual to decadal timescales. The practical difficulties are specific and instructive: initialising a component you barely observe, model drift away from the observed state, and the need for large re-forecast sets before any skill claim can be believed.

## The planned notebook

A minimal two-timescale system — fast 'weather' coupled to a slow 'ocean' — where initialising the slow component demonstrably extends predictability of the slow variable while doing nothing for the fast one.
