## Overview

The question the whole book has been circling: if weather is unpredictable beyond two weeks, on what basis is a projection for 2100 credible? The answer is that the two are different questions. Weather forecasting asks for the trajectory on the attractor; climate projection asks how the attractor itself — its statistics — responds to a change in forcing. The chapter makes that distinction precise and shows where it breaks down.

## The planned notebook

The same low-order system run as an initial-value problem and as a forced-response problem, so the reader sees the trajectory become unpredictable while the statistics stay predictable.
