## Overview

Extending prediction to the carbon cycle, the cryosphere, biogeochemistry and land — components with their own timescales, their own sparse observations, and in some cases their own instabilities. What does it even mean to initialise a component whose adjustment time exceeds the forecast? A survey chapter, following the course's guest lectures on frontiers in Earth system predictability.

## The planned notebook

No single minimal model does this justice; the chapter uses a coupled fast-slow-slower cascade to illustrate timescale separation and where initialisation stops paying.
