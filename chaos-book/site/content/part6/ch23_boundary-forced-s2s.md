## Overview

Beyond the deterministic weather limit, and before the climate-response regime, lies the subseasonal-to-seasonal range — long called a predictability desert. It is not empty. Slowly varying boundary conditions and slow internal modes (ENSO, the MJO, sudden stratospheric warmings, monsoon onset, soil moisture) confer predictability that has nothing to do with knowing today's synoptic state: predictability of the second kind.

## The planned notebook

Lorenz 63 with a slowly varying $\rho$: the trajectory is as unpredictable as ever, but the *statistics* conditioned on the forcing phase are strongly predictable — the mechanism of second-kind predictability in its smallest form.
