## Overview

Some systems have more than one attractor, and a slowly changing parameter can destroy the one the system currently occupies. That is a tipping point, and the prospect of anticipating one has produced a large literature on early-warning indicators — rising variance and autocorrelation as a bifurcation approaches. The chapter presents both the theory and its well-documented failure modes.

## The planned notebook

A stochastic double-well system: the reader varies the noise and the forcing rate and tries to call the transition in advance from variance and lag-1 autocorrelation alone — and discovers how often the warning arrives too late.
