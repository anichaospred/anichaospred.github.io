---
title: "An Interactive Chaos and Predictability Textbook"
---

Why weather forecasts fail beyond about two weeks, why climate projections do not, and
what deterministic chaos has to say about the limits of prediction in the atmosphere and
ocean.

Every chapter pairs a short piece of text with **one interactive notebook that runs in
your browser** — no installation, no server, no account. The numerics execute in your own
tab via Pyodide, so you can move a slider and watch the forecast horizon move with it.

The book is organised around a single idea, borrowed from the course it accompanies:
*apply a hierarchy of models to understand predictability in nonlinear dynamical systems.*
Predictability is never one number. It is a property of a system, a state, a lead time and
a question — and the only convincing way to learn that is to turn the knob yourself.
