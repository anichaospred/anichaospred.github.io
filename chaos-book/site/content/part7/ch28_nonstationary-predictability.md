## Overview

Forecast skill has improved steadily for fifty years, but that conflates two different things: better models and observations, and any change in the predictability of the atmosphere itself. If the circulation is changing, the intrinsic limit may be moving too. Separating the two requires care, and the chapter is mostly about how that separation is attempted and how it can fail.

## The planned notebook

Lorenz 96 with a slowly increasing forcing: skill measured with a fixed 'model' changes even though nothing about the forecasting system did — the confound in its cleanest form.
