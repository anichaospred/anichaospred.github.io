## Overview

Exponential growth is only the small-error limit. Real forecast errors saturate at the climatological difference between two randomly chosen atmospheric states, and the approach to saturation — not the doubling time — is what sets the useful horizon. Lorenz's logistic model captures both regimes with one extra parameter, and it changes the answer: halving the initial error does not buy a fixed doubling time's worth of extra lead when the error is already large.
