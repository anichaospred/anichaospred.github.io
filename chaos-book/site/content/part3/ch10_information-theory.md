## Overview

The course summary names information theory explicitly, and it answers a question the dynamical view cannot: *how much does this forecast tell us that climatology did not?* Relative entropy between the forecast and climatological distributions is the natural currency, and it separates cleanly into a **signal** component (the forecast mean differs from climatology) and a **dispersion** component (the forecast is sharper). Neither requires Gaussianity or exponential growth.
